Citing
======

If you use arcwind, please cite the software itself and Louvet et al. (2018,
A&A 618, A120), whose method for HH 30 it generalizes. The citation metadata for
arcwind are in ``CITATION.cff`` at the root of the repository; on GitHub, the
"Cite this repository" button gives them as BibTeX or APA. arcwind relies on NumPy (Harris et al. 2020),
SciPy (Virtanen et al. 2020) and Matplotlib (Hunter 2007).

The use of AI assistance in developing arcwind, and the authors' review of it,
is described under :doc:`contributing` (Acknowledgments).
